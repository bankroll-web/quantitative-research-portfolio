# BTC Quantitative Market Ecology Research

## Objective

Test whether Bitcoin price behavior can be better understood by modeling distinct participant groups as separate components of a market ecology rather than treating the market as one monolithic signal.

## Background

This research grew out of nine years of hands-on crypto trading experience and a transition toward systematic, math-first research. The goal is not to encode discretionary chart patterns, but to construct falsifiable hypotheses using participant data, statistics, probability, machine learning, and forward validation.

## Phase 1 — Retail Ecology

**Status: Complete research phase / continuing validation**

- FXSSI retail positioning used as the primary participant signal source.
- Approximately 26 numbered experiments were run to test and refine hypotheses about retail positioning and BTC price behavior.
- Research included chronology and candidate-mechanism studies across approximately 20-120 minute horizons.
- Findings were synthesized into a working model of the relationship between retail reallocation and BTC price impulses.

## Phase 2 — Institutional Ecology

**Status: In progress**

The next layer seeks a data source that can represent institutional participant behavior independently from the retail signal. The intended test is whether separately validated ecology layers provide more explanatory or predictive information together than either layer alone.

## Validation Philosophy

The project deliberately separates historical discovery from prospective evidence.

Historical tests produced positive findings, but forward/live validation did not initially reproduce the historical performance. Rather than optimizing the discrepancy away, the project treats it as a research result and investigates candidate causes including:

- data leakage
- regime shift
- measurement semantics
- execution assumptions
- feature timing
- model calibration

This distinction between backtest performance and prospective evidence is central to the project.

## Methods

- Statistical and probabilistic hypothesis testing
- Walk-forward validation
- Calibration
- ML ensembles
- Chronology/mechanism testing
- Prospective paper validation
- Explicit negative-result documentation

## Current Direction

Build and independently validate an institutional ecology layer, then test whether retail + institutional layers improve the overall participant model without introducing leakage or post-hoc optimization.

## Research Standard

No result is presented as a deployable trading edge solely because it performed well historically. Claims are intentionally bounded by the available out-of-sample and forward evidence.
